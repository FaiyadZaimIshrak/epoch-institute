# Earning from the Institute: the owner's checklist and log

The site stays free: no paywall and no content held back. Income comes from readers who choose to support it, from
products made of the Institute's own work (teaching packs, prints, the essays as a book), from book links, and later
from advertising. The plan, in four phases, is in the project notes (CLAUDE.md, "Earning from the Institute").

## Rules that keep it safe

- **"Support", never "donate":** the Institute is run by an individual, not a charity.
- **Products are made only of the Institute's own work:** its maps (Natural Earth outlines, public domain), data,
  timelines, quizzes and essays. Never sell the encyclopedia's text (adapted from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA) or Wikimedia
  Commons pictures.
- **The three Sumerian song texts** (Gilgamesh, the Hymn to Ninkasi, the Kesh Temple Hymn) are licensed for
  non-commercial use only. They never go into a product, and their pages never show ads.
- **Kids mode and the Kids Corner never show ads or prompts to pay.**

## Done

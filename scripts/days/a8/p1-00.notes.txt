Notes on check-events.js flags for p1-00.json (15 remaining, all checked by hand)

The checker joins the title and description with a space, so a description's first word gets read as part of
the title's last capitalised name ("Doraha Sheltering", "Great Britain Meeting", "Kingdom of Jerusalem
Ecclesiastical", "Pope Clement IV After", "Saint Thomas Christians | They", "Soon", "Little", "Its"). Each of the
names on its own is in the material (Doraha, Great Britain, Kingdom of Jerusalem, Pope Clement IV).

Possessives and plurals of names in the article: Hadım Suleiman Pasha's, Constans's, Confederacy's (On this day
line), Japan's, Fastolf's, Lady Chapel's, Isa ibn Musa's (Abbasid is in the article), Spain's / Admiral Miguel de
Horna's Armada of Flanders ("Spanish admiral Miguel de Horna, commander of the Armada of Flanders"),
Afro-Brazilians ("Afro-Brazilian people"), Saint Thomas Christians ("Saint Thomas Christian community").

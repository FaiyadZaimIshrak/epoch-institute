== story
"Chhaap Tilak Sab Chheeni" is a 14th-century song in Braj Bhasha. It is credited to the Sufi mystic Amir Khusrau as both poet and composer, and is one of his most popular compositions, heard across the Indian subcontinent.

It is sung above all as qawwali, the devotional music of the Sufis, by Pakistani and Indian qawwals and other singers. Those who have performed it include Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Naheed Akhtar, Mehnaz Begum, Abida Parveen, the Sabri Brothers, Iqbal Hussain Khan Bandanawazi, Farid Ayaz and Abu Muhammad Qawwal, Jafar Hussain Khan, the sitar and sarod players Ustad Vilayat Khan and Ustad Shujaat Khan, Zila Khan, the Nizami Bandhu, Hadiqa Kiani, Smita Bellur, Lata Mangeshkar, Asha Bhosle, Rahat Fateh Ali Khan, Kailash Kher, Kavita Seth and Maithili Thakur.

The song also passed into film and television. Lata Mangeshkar and Asha Bhosle sang a version in the 1978 Hindi film Main Tulsi Tere Aangan Ki, and Abida Parveen and Rahat Fateh Ali Khan sang another on the Pakistani music show Coke Studio. It was sung by Keerthi Sagathia, in a setting by Bapi–Tutul, in the film Saat Uchakkey (2016), and by Shishir A Samant and Sunil Kamath in Unpaused (2020).
== meaning
A Sufi song of devotion in which the singer describes being utterly won over by a single glance of the beloved, so that the outward marks of identity and religion fall away. The beloved is read in the Sufi way as the spiritual guide or God. The mood is rapturous and surrendering.

== story
"Ko e fasi ʻo e tuʻi ʻo e ʻOtu Tonga" is the national anthem of Tonga. Its title means "Song of the King of the Tonga Islands"; when the Tongan monarch is a woman it is sung as "Ko e fasi ʻo e kuini ʻo e ʻOtu Tonga", "Song of the Queen of the Tonga Islands". In everyday life it is better known as "Fasi fakafonua", the "National Song".

The words were written by the Tongan prince Uelingatoni Ngū Tupoumalohi and the music by Carl Gustav Schmitt, a German-born composer of New Zealand. The anthem was first used in 1874. Its music is printed both in Western notation and in tuʻungafasi, the Tongan system of music notation.
== meaning
A royal anthem: it addresses the monarch of the Tonga Islands, and its title changes with whether a king or a queen reigns. The mood is solemn and loyal.

== story
"Chakrulo" is a Georgian polyphonic choral folk song from the region of Kakheti. It is sung in three parts and dramatises the preparations for a battle. Two highly ornamented solo voices sing over a choral foundation.

In 1977 it was one of the 27 pieces of music on the Voyager Golden Records, sent into space aboard Voyager 2 on 20 August 1977 and Voyager 1 on 5 September 1977. When UNESCO recognised Georgian vocal polyphony as a masterpiece of Intangible Heritage in 2001, "Chakrulo" was cited as a prime example.
== meaning
A song of men readying themselves for battle. Its mood is grave and resolute, carried by two richly ornamented voices above a steady chorus.

== story
The Cherifian Anthem is the national anthem of Morocco. Its melody was composed by Léo Morgan, a French military officer who was chief of music for the Moroccan Royal Guard, during the French protectorate. It is believed to have been composed in 1952, four years before Morocco's independence in 1956, and the anthem has been in use since the protectorate period.

The words came later. After the Moroccan national football team qualified for the FIFA World Cup for the first time, for the 1970 tournament, the idea of writing lyrics for the anthem was proposed. The lyrics were written by the Moroccan author and poet Professor Ali Squalli Houssaini and adopted in 1970. Accounts differ on how they were chosen: either King Hassan II picked them in a competition, or he commissioned Squalli Houssaini to write them directly.

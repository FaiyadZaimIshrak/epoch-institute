== story
"Kagome Kagome" is a Japanese children's game and the song (warabe uta) that goes with it. One player is chosen as the Oni, a word that means a demon or ogre but here plays the part of "it" in tag, and sits blindfolded or with eyes covered. The other children join hands and walk in a circle around the Oni while they sing. When the song stops, the Oni tries to name the child standing directly behind.

Who wrote the words and the tune, and what the words were first meant to say, is not known. The song is usually written as a single line without punctuation, its phrasing is odd and several of its words have more than one meaning, so it is unclear which phrases belong together. The words also vary from region to region, and many variants have been recorded in writing over time.

Its puzzling words have drawn much interest from scholars and the public, and many readings of each phrase have been proposed. The opening word kagome has been read as the woven pattern of a bamboo basket, as a corruption of words meaning "surround" or "crouch" called out in the game, as a hexagon or hexagram, as a "basket woman", meaning a pregnant woman, and as a shrine maiden. The theory adopted by Japanese dictionaries holds that the words describe the game alone, the creature shut in being the child who is "it"; even there, one phrase is often explained only as filling the rhythm.

Other theories read darker stories into the song: the sorrow of a prostitute without freedom, a pregnant woman pushed on the stairs during a quarrel over an inheritance, a prisoner awaiting escape or execution, or a person's spiritual awakening. None of them has been proved, and children across Japan still play the game with the song.
== meaning
The words are a riddle sung around the blindfolded child. They speak of something shut in and waiting to come out, set at a time of day that cannot exist, and end by challenging the child in the middle to guess who stands behind. Their play on words with more than one meaning leaves the song open to many readings.

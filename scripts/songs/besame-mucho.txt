== story
"Bésame Mucho" is a bolero written in 1932 by the Mexican songwriter Consuelo Velázquez. By her own account she wrote it before she had ever been kissed, at a time when kissing, as she had heard, was considered a sin. She took her inspiration from "Quejas, o la Maja y el Ruiseñor", a piano piece in the suite Goyescas (1911) by the Spanish composer Enrique Granados, which he later used as the "Aria of the Nightingale" in his opera of 1916. English lyrics were later written by Sunny Skylar.

The song reached a wide audience in 1944. It was played by Charlie Spivak and his Orchestra in the film Follow the Boys (5 May 1944) and sung by Dale Evans in Cowboy and the Senorita (13 May 1944), and a version by Bob Eberly and Kitty Kallen with Jimmy Dorsey and His Orchestra reached number one in the United States that year. As it crossed the Atlantic in 1944 it became one of the most recognisable melodies of its time. Velázquez herself recorded it as a piano instrumental on Piano Interpretations (1955).

It became a pop and jazz standard and one of the most important songs in the history of Latin music; in 1999 it was recognised as the most recorded and covered Spanish-language song of all time. Among its many singers were Pedro Vargas, Trio Los Panchos, Ray Conniff, whose 1960 recording became a worldwide hit, the Beatles, who played it at their Decca audition on 1 January 1962 and at their first Abbey Road session on 6 June 1962, Dalida, who took it to number five in France in 1976, João Gilberto, Plácido Domingo and Luis Miguel.

In 1990 the song was at the centre of a Brazilian political scandal. A hidden affair between two government ministers, Bernardo Cabral and Zélia Cardoso de Mello, became public when they danced cheek to cheek to "Bésame Mucho" at her birthday party, and Cabral, a married father of three, was forced to resign. A few days later, when Cardoso de Mello received the Order of Military Merit, the band of the presidential guard, the Dragões da Independência, struck up the song; its musical director, Lt. Geraldo Mendonça da Lima, was given three days' detention for insubordination.
== meaning
A love song of longing and tenderness. Its mood is warm and romantic, with an undertone of yearning.

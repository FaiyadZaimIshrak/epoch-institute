== story
"Hine E Hine" is a lullaby in Māori written in around 1907 by Fanny Howie, who performed under the stage name Princess Te Rangi Pai. It was published in London by Beal, Stuttard & Co., around 1914, as "A Maori Slumber Song – Hine E Hine", under her stage name; a copy is held by the Alexander Turnbull Library of the National Library of New Zealand.

From 1975 to 1994 the television channel TV2 used an instrumental version as its closedown song, played with a cartoon of the Goodnight Kiwi. Singers have kept it in the repertoire since. It opened Kiri Te Kanawa's 1999 album Maori Songs, Hayley Westenra sang it on her 2003 album Pure, and it appears on the album The José Carreras Collection. The Phoenix Foundation performed it in the 2010 film Boy.
== meaning
A lullaby, published as a "slumber song": a song sung to settle a child to sleep. Its mood is quiet and gentle.

== story
"Jai Jai Garavi Gujarat" is a poem written in 1873 by the Gujarati poet Narmadashankar Dave, known as Narmad, who is considered the first modern Gujarati writer. He wrote it as the foreword to his first Gujarati dictionary, the Narmakosh. In it he gave voice to pride in the region by drawing the bounds of the land where the Gujarati-speaking people lived: Ambaji in the north, Pavagadh in the east, Kunteshwar Mahadev near Vapi in the south, and Somnath and Dwarka in the west. The region he outlined now forms the modern state of Gujarat in western India.

The poem is used as a state anthem at ceremonies of the Government of Gujarat. In 2011 the government released a recording of it sung by a number of Gujarati singers.
== meaning
A poem of pride in Gujarat: it salutes the land of the Gujarati people, names the holy places at its four corners, and ends in hope that the dark clouds over Gujarat are lifting and a new dawn is near. Its mood is proud and hopeful.

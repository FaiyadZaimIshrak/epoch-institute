== story
"E Ipo" ("My Darling") is a song of 1982 written by Prince Tui Teka and Ngoi Pēwhairangi in tribute to Teka's wife, Missy, and sung in both Māori and English. Its melody was based on "Mimpi Sedih", a popular Indonesian love song by Aloysius Riyanto, which Teka had heard from New Zealand soldiers stationed in Singapore while he was performing overseas between 1980 and 1982.

The song was a number-one single in New Zealand for two weeks. Its video was taken from a television special filmed at the Mandalay nightclub in Newmarket, Auckland, and it was later included in Nature's Best 3, a collection of the top 100 New Zealand songs as voted by members of APRA. "E Ipo" and "Poi E" (1984) by the Pātea Māori Club, also written by Pēwhairangi, were the first widely successful songs sung in te reo Māori in mainstream music, and they did much to promote the Māori language and Māori culture in New Zealand.
== meaning
A love song from a husband to his wife, tender and devoted, moving between Māori and English.

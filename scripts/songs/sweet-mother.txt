== story
"Sweet Mother" is a highlife song by the Cameroonian and Nigerian singer Prince Nico Mbarga and his band Rocafil Jazz. Its demo tape was turned down by EMI in 1974, which cited the song's "childish appeal", and it was later rejected by Decca and Philips Records too. It was finally released in December 1976 by Rogers All Stars, a Nigerian recording company based in Onitsha. The song is sung in Nigerian Pidgin English; its music is West African highlife, with guitar finger-picking in the style of Congolese soukous.

"Sweet Mother" became one of the most popular hits in Africa, selling between 3 and 13 million copies, and it is sometimes called "Africa's anthem". In 2004 the readers and listeners of the BBC voted it Africa's favourite song, ahead of Brenda Fassie's "Vuli Ndlela", Fela Kuti's "Lady", Franco's "Mario" and Miriam Makeba's version of "Malaika".
== meaning
A celebration of motherhood: a song of love and thanks to one's mother, warm and joyful in mood.

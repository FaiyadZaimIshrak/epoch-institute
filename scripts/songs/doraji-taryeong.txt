== story
"Doraji taryeong" is a Korean folk song that began in Eunyul, in Hwanghae province. The version sung today is counted among the folk songs of Gyeonggi, because its rhythm and melody changed over time and took on the character of that region's songs.

Its title names the doraji, the Korean name for the plant Platycodon grandiflorus, the balloon flower, and for its root. The song is set to the fast rhythmic pattern (jangdan) called semachi, at times switching to the jungmori pattern, and like other traditional Korean songs it uses a five-note scale.

It is one of the most popular folk songs in both North and South Korea and among the Koreans of China, and it is well known in Japan under the name Toraji.
== meaning
A light folk song built around the doraji, the balloon flower and its root. The mood is lively, carried by its fast dance-like rhythm.

== story
"Ee Mungu Nguvu Yetu" (literally "O God, our strength"; in English "O God of all Creation") is the national anthem of Kenya. It was written in 1963 by the Kenyan Anthem Commission, a body of five members headed by the Kenya Music Adviser, to serve as the state anthem after Kenya's independence from the United Kingdom. It was one of the first national anthems to be specifically commissioned.

The words were written in Kiswahili, the national language of Kenya, and were meant to express the deepest convictions and highest aspirations of the people. The music is the traditional part: the commission based the anthem on a traditional tune sung by Pokomo mothers to their children.
== meaning
A prayer to God, as its title says, for the new nation, written to voice the convictions and hopes of the people of independent Kenya. Its mood is solemn and hopeful.

== story
"Kangding Qingge", the "Kangding Love Song", is a traditional folk song of Kangding, in Sichuan Province, and one of the most popular songs across the Sinosphere.

In 1946 Wu Wen-ji, a native of Quanzhou who was studying vocal music in Sikang Province, collected a local folk song called "Paoma Liuliude Shanshang" ("On the Running Horse Mountain") among others. While teaching at a Kuomintang military academy he wrote out its score and renamed it "Kangding Love Song", after Kangding, the capital of Sikang Province. The soprano Yu Yixuan, popular at the time, performed it, and through her it spread to the rest of China.

It has since been performed or recorded by many artists, among them Plácido Domingo, Timi Zhuo and A-mei. It has been sampled and reworked in popular music, film, television and video games: Karen Mok's "The Smooth Love Song" samples it, Tan Weiwei remixed it on the television contest I Am a Singer in 2015, Sammo Hung sings part of it in the film Dragons Forever, and it is heard in an episode of the television series Daredevil.
== meaning
A folk love song, named after the town of Kangding. Its mood is light and lilting.

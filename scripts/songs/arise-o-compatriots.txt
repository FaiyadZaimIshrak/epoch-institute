== story
"Arise, O Compatriots" was the national anthem of Nigeria from 1 October 1978 until 2024. It was adopted in 1978 by the military government of the time and replaced the country's first anthem, "Nigeria, We Hail Thee", which had been in use since 1960.

Its words were not written by a single author. They combine words and phrases taken from five of the best entries in a national contest, by P. O. Aderibigbe, John A. Ilechukwu, Dr. Sota Omoigui, Eme Etim Akpan and B. A. Ogunnaike. The Nigeria Police Band, under the direction of Benedict P. Odiase (1934–2013), set them to music.

The anthem has two verses, but usually only the first was sung; on some occasions the second was recited as "The National Prayer". The national pledge, written by Felicia Adebola Adeyoyin in 1976, was recited immediately after the anthem was played.

In 2024 the National Assembly passed a bill restoring "Nigeria, We Hail Thee", and President Bola Tinubu signed it into law. On 29 May 2024 "Arise, O Compatriots" was officially given up and the first anthem readopted.

A patriotic anthem calling the citizens to the service of their country; solemn and dedicated in mood.

== story
Rabindranath Tagore wrote "Ekla Chalo Re" in 1905 at Giridih, in present-day Jharkhand. It was one of 22 protest songs he wrote in the Swadeshi period of the Indian freedom movement, and with "Amar Sonar Bangla" it became one of the key songs of the movement against the partition of the Bengal Presidency in 1905. It was based on and influenced by "Harinaam Diye Jagat Matale Amar Ekla Nitai Re", a popular Bengali Vaishnavite kirtan of the Dhapkirtan or Manoharshahi gharana in praise of Nityananda, disciple of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.

Titled "Eka" ("Alone"), it was first published in the September 1905 issue of the magazine Bhandar and included the same year in Tagore's song anthology Baul. In 1941 it was placed in the "Swadesh" ("Homeland") section of Gitabitan, the complete anthology of Tagore's songs. Its musical notation was prepared by Indira Devi, Tagore's niece, first published in the April–May 1906 issue of Sangeet-Vignan Prakashika and later included in the 46th volume of Swarabitan, the complete collection of Tagore's notations.

Tagore himself first recorded the song sometime between 1905 and 1908, on a cylinder labelled H. Bose Swadeshi Records that is now lost. Two other early records were made by Harendranath Dutta, for the Gramophone Company of India, and by the Hindustan Party (Amala Dutta, Nandita Devi, Sudhin Dutta and Santidev Ghosh), for Hindustan Records. The Rabindra Sangeet singer Suchitra Mitra recorded it four times: in 1948 for the film Sandipan Pathshala, in 1984, on the album Rupantori (1988), and with the sarod of Ustad Amjad Ali Khan on Tribute to Tagore.

The song urges the listener to go on with their journey even when others abandon them or give no support. It was a favourite of Mahatma Gandhi and is often quoted in movements for social and political change. Its tune was used with Hindi words in the 2004 film Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose: The Forgotten Hero, and Amitabh Bachchan sang it in the 2012 film Kahaani.
== meaning
A song of resolve: if no one answers your call or walks with you, you should still go forward alone. The mood is steady and defiant rather than sad.

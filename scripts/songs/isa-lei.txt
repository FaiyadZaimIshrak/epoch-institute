== story
"Isa Lei" is a traditional Fijian farewell song, and its origin is disputed. By one account, Ratu Tevita Uluilakeba, the Turaga Bale na Tu'i Nayau, composed it in 1916 for Adi Litia Tavanavanua (1900–1983) when she visited Tubou, on Lakeba. The Fiji Museum holds Uluilakeba's manuscript, but by the museum's description he composed the song in 1918, while he was training as a civil servant in Suva. Uluilakeba was the father of Ratu Sir Kamisese Mara, the founding father of modern Fiji.

By another account, "Isa Lei" is the Fijian version of a Tongan love song written in 1915 to court the then Princess Salote, later Queen Sālote Tupou III. A visiting Fijian sergeant heard it, and the Fijians turned it into a farewell song while keeping the Tongan melody.

In 1932 Lieutenant A. W. Caten, a bandmaster of the Fijian Defence Forces, made a foxtrot arrangement of the song. He is often credited on modern recordings of it, among them versions by The Seekers and by Ry Cooder and V. M. Bhatt on their album A Meeting by the River. The Fijian singer Sakiusa Bulicokocoko performed it regularly.
== meaning
A farewell song of parting and of the wish to be remembered, drawn by one account from a love song. Its mood is tender and wistful.

== story
"All Hail, Liberia, Hail!" is the national anthem of Liberia. Its words were written by Daniel Bashiel Warner (1815–1880), who later became the third president of Liberia, and its music was composed by Olmstead Luca (1826–1869). It became the official national anthem when Liberia became independent in 1847.

In the 1970s the anthem was reconsidered along with the country's other national symbols. On 22 July 1974 the Legislature of Liberia passed an act allowing the president to set up a commission to consider changes to a number of national symbols, among them the anthem and the flag. President William Tolbert appointed 51 members to the Commission on National Unity, headed by McKinley Alfred Deshield Sr. and also called the Deshield Commission, which set out to re-examine the symbols and remove what divided Liberians. Its report, submitted on 24 January 1978, recommended changing one word of the anthem. The change was never made, and the anthem is sung with its original words.
== meaning
A patriotic hymn that hails Liberia as a free nation and calls its people to unity and loyalty. The mood is proud and solemn.

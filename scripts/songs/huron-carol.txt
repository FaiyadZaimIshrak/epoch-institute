== story
The "Huron Carol" is a Christmas hymn, counted as Canada's oldest Christmas song. It was written, probably in 1642, by Jean de Brébeuf, a Jesuit missionary at Sainte-Marie among the Hurons, in the language of the Huron-Wendat people. Its Wendat title is "Jesous Ahatonhia", "Jesus, he is born". The tune is that of a traditional French folk song, "Une Jeune Pucelle" ("A Young Maid").

The carol remains a common Christmas hymn in Canadian churches of many denominations, and it stands in several American hymnals, among them The Hymnal 1982 of the Episcopal Church (No. 114), The United Methodist Hymnal (No. 244) and Evangelical Lutheran Worship (No. 284). Its tune keeps to a narrow range, which suits instruments of limited compass such as the Native American flute.

The carol has been recorded many times, sometimes in the original Wendat: by Burl Ives in 1952 as "Jesous Ahatonia", by Bruce Cockburn in Huron, and by Heather Dale in 2002 in Wendat, French and English.
== meaning
A Christmas carol telling of the birth of Jesus for a Wendat congregation, in their own language. Its tone is gentle and joyful, close to the folk song whose tune it borrows.

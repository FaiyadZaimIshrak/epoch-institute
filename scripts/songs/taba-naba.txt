== story
"Taba Naba" is a traditional children's song of the Torres Strait Islands, just north of the Australian mainland, sung in Meriam Mir, a language of the Torres Strait Islanders. It is usually accompanied by a "sit-down dance", in which the dancers stay seated and perform traditional movements that match the words.

The song reached a wide audience when the children's band The Wiggles performed it with the Australian pop singer Christine Anu, a native of the eastern Torres Strait, on their album It's a Wiggly Wiggly World (2000); in the video Anu performed the sit-down dance. It was later featured in two of the band's television series, Lights, Camera, Action, Wiggles! and Ready, Steady, Wiggle!, and in Wiggle Town and Duets, with Anu returning. In Brazil the singer Xuxa recorded it for her album Xuxa Só Para Baixinhos 4.
== meaning
An invitation to go out to the reef: the singers call one another to get into the dinghy when the morning tide is low and row out to the reef's edge. The seated dancers act out the words with their arms and hands. The mood is cheerful and lively.

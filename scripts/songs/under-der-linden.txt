== story
"Under der linden" is a well-known poem by the medieval German lyric poet Walther von der Vogelweide, written in Middle High German. It was a song: its four strophes are preserved in only two manuscripts, the Weingarten Manuscript and the Manesse Codex.

Neither manuscript holds a melody, so the tune Walther's audience heard is not known. The melody of an anonymous Old French song, "En mai au douz tens novels", fits the metre of the poem, and this suggests that "Under der linden" may be a contrafactum: new words written to an existing French tune. One word of the poem imitates the song of the nightingale.
== meaning
A song of four strophes, in which one word imitates the song of the nightingale. The mood is light and musical.

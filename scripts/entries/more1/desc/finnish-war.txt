name: Finnish War
type: event (war)
dates: 1808–1809

The Finnish War was fought from 1808 to 1809 between Sweden and the Russian Empire, which invaded Finland after Sweden refused to join Napoleon's Continental System. Russian forces took the fortress of Sveaborg and overran Finland. By the Treaty of Fredrikshamn of 1809 Sweden ceded Finland, which became an autonomous grand duchy under the Russian tsar.

name: Ambhi
type: person
dates: 4th century BCE

Ambhi was the king of Taxila in north-western India in the late 4th century BCE, known to the Greeks as Taxiles and also as Omphis. A rival of the neighbouring Paurava king Porus, he submitted to Alexander the Great in 326 BCE and supplied troops and elephants for the campaign against Porus at the Hydaspes. Alexander confirmed him as ruler of his kingdom.

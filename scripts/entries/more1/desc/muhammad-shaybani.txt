name: Muhammad Shaybani
type: person
dates: c. 1451–1510

Muhammad Shaybani was the leader of the Uzbeks who conquered Transoxiana and Khorasan in the early sixteenth century. He drove the Timurids from Samarkand and Herat, ending the Timurid Empire. He was defeated and killed by the Safavid shah Ismail I in 1510.

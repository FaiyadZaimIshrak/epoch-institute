name: Peter Tordenskjold
type: person
dates: 1690–1720

Peter Tordenskjold, born Peter Wessel in Trondheim, was a Norwegian-born naval officer in the service of Denmark-Norway during the Great Northern War. Ennobled in 1716, he won fame through bold actions such as the destruction of a Swedish transport fleet at Dynekilen in 1716 and the capture of Marstrand in 1719. He was killed in a duel in 1720.

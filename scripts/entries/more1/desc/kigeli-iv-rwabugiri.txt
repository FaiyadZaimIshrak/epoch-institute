name: Kigeli IV Rwabugiri
type: person
dates: c. 1840–1895

Kigeli IV Rwabugiri was mwami (king) of Rwanda in the second half of the 19th century and is regarded as its most powerful pre-colonial ruler. He centralised royal authority, weakened lineage chiefs, extended the system of cattle clientage and led many military campaigns against neighbouring lands. He died in 1895, shortly before Rwanda came under German rule.

name: Jin–Song wars
type: event (war)
dates: 1125–1234

The Jin–Song wars were a series of conflicts fought from 1125 to 1234 between the Jurchen-led Jin dynasty and the Song dynasty of China. In the Jingkang incident of 1127 the Jin took the Song capital Kaifeng and captured two emperors, forcing the Song court south to Hangzhou. Generals such as Yue Fei resisted, and the Treaty of Shaoxing (1142) fixed the border on the Huai River; later wars changed little before the Mongols destroyed the Jin in 1234.

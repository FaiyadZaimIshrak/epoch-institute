name: Bodleian Library
type: institution
dates: 1602

The Bodleian Library is the main research library of the University of Oxford, opened in 1602 through the refounding of the university library by Sir Thomas Bodley. One of the oldest libraries in Europe and a legal deposit library, it holds millions of books and manuscripts, among them the only known palm-leaf manuscript of the Old Sundanese poem of Bujangga Manik, held since 1627.

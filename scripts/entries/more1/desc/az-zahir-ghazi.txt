name: Az-Zahir Ghazi
type: person
dates: 1172–1216

Az-Zahir Ghazi was a son of Saladin and Ayyubid ruler of Aleppo from 1186 to 1216. He kept his principality independent of his uncle al-Adil, strengthened Aleppo's citadel and built mosques and madrasas, making the city a centre of learning. His descendants ruled Aleppo until the Mongol conquest of 1260.

The Majeerteen are a Somali clan of the Darod family, living mainly in the north-east of Somalia, in Puntland. In the 18th and 19th centuries their leaders ruled the Majeerteen Sultanate and the Sultanate of Hobyo, which accepted Italian protection and were conquered by Italy in the 1920s. The clan has remained politically prominent, and Puntland was founded largely on its territory.

name: Andhra Mahabharatam
type: work (text)
dates: 11th–14th century

The Andhra Mahabharatam is the Telugu version of the Mahabharata, written by three poets known as the Kavitrayam. Nannaya began it in the 11th century at the court of the Eastern Chalukya king Rajaraja Narendra, Tikkana continued it in the 13th century, and Errana completed it in the 14th century under the Reddi kings. It is regarded as the founding work of Telugu literature.

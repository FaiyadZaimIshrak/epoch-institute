name: Jimena Díaz
type: person
dates: c. 1046–c. 1116

Jimena Díaz was the wife of Rodrigo Díaz de Vivar, El Cid, and a relative of King Alfonso VI of León and Castile. After the Cid's death in 1099 she governed Valencia and defended it against the Almoravids until 1102, when Alfonso evacuated the city and burned it. She then took her husband's body to the monastery of San Pedro de Cardeña.

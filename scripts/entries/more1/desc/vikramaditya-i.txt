name: Vikramaditya I
type: person
dates: r. c. 655–680

Vikramaditya I was king of the Chalukyas of Badami from about 655 to 680, son of Pulakeshin II. After the Pallavas had captured Badami in 642 and the kingdom fell into disorder, he expelled them, reunited the Chalukya lands and later raided the Pallava capital Kanchipuram. His successors kept the dynasty dominant in the Deccan until 753.

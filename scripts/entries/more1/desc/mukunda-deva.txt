name: Mukunda Deva
type: person
dates: r. 1559–1568

Mukunda Deva was the last independent Hindu king of Odisha, ruling from about 1559 to 1568. A minister of the Bhoi dynasty, he seized the throne by overthrowing its last rulers. In 1568 he was killed in a war with rebels while the Karrani sultans of Bengal invaded, and Odisha passed under Afghan and later Mughal rule.

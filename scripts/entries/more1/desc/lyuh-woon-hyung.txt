name: Lyuh Woon-hyung
type: person
dates: 1886–1947

Lyuh Woon-hyung was a Korean independence activist and moderate leftist politician. On Japan's surrender in August 1945 he formed the Committee for the Preparation of Korean Independence, which proclaimed the People's Republic of Korea in September, but the American occupation refused to recognise it. He then worked for cooperation between left and right until he was assassinated in Seoul in 1947.

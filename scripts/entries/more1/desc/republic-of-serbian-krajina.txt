name: Republic of Serbian Krajina
type: polity
dates: 1991–1995

The Republic of Serbian Krajina was a self-proclaimed Serb state in Croatia, declared in 1991 by Croatian Serbs opposed to Croatian independence and backed by the Yugoslav army and Serbia. It covered about a third of Croatia, including the old Military Frontier lands, with its capital at Knin, and expelled much of its Croat population. It was overrun by the Croatian army in Operation Storm in August 1995, when most of its Serb inhabitants fled.

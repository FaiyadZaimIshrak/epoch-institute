name: Sadashivrao Bhau
type: person
dates: 1730–1761

Sadashivrao Bhau was a Maratha general and cousin of the Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao. He defeated the Nizam of Hyderabad at Udgir in 1760, gaining large territories, and then commanded the Maratha army against Ahmad Shah Durrani. He was killed at the Third Battle of Panipat in 1761.

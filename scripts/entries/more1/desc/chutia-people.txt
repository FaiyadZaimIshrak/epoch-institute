The Chutia are an ethnic group of Assam in north-eastern India, of Bodo-Kachari origin. Their medieval kingdom, centred on Sadiya in the upper Brahmaputra valley, flourished from about the 13th century until the Ahom king Suhungmung annexed it in 1523–24. Many Chutias were then absorbed into Ahom society, and today they form a recognised community speaking mainly Assamese.

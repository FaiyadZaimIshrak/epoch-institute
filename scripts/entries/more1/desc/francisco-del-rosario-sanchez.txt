name: Francisco del Rosario Sánchez
type: person
dates: 1817–1861

Francisco del Rosario Sánchez was a Dominican independence leader and member of La Trinitaria, the secret society of Juan Pablo Duarte. On 27 February 1844 he proclaimed independence from Haiti at the Puerta del Conde in Santo Domingo and raised the new flag. When Pedro Santana annexed the country to Spain in 1861, Sánchez led a rising against it and was captured and shot.

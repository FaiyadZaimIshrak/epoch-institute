name: Manghud
type: group
dates: none

The Manghud, or Mangit, were a Mongol tribe that became Turkic-speaking in the Golden Horde. Under the emir Edigu around 1400 they formed the core of the Nogai Horde, and other Mangit clans joined the Uzbek confederation of Abu'l-Khayr Khan. A Mangit family later founded the Manghit dynasty, which ruled the Emirate of Bukhara until 1920.

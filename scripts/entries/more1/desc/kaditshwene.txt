name: Kaditshwene
type: place (site)
dates: none

Kaditshwene was the capital of the Bahurutshe, a large stone-walled Tswana town on a hill near present-day Zeerust in South Africa's North West province. When the missionary John Campbell visited it in 1820 he estimated its population at 16,000 and admired its metalworking and trade. It was destroyed in the wars of the early 1820s, and its stone ruins are studied by archaeologists.

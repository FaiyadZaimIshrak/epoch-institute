name: Hö'elün
type: person
dates: none

Hö'elün was the mother of Temüjin, later Genghis Khan. According to the Secret History of the Mongols, she was seized by Yesügei from her Merkit husband, and after Yesügei's death she raised her children in poverty when their clan abandoned them. She remained an influential adviser to Genghis Khan and adopted several orphans who became Mongol commanders.

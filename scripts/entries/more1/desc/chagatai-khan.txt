name: Chagatai Khan
type: person
dates: r. 1227–1242

Chagatai Khan was the second son of Genghis Khan, who received a share of the Mongol Empire in Central Asia and ruled from 1227 to 1242. His territories included Transoxiana, the Ili River valley and the Tarim Basin, formerly ruled by the Qara Khitai. The Chagatai Empire, or Chagatai Khanate, was named after him.

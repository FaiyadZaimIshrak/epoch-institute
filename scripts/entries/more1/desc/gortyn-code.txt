name: Gortyn Code
type: work (text)
dates: c. 450 BCE

The Gortyn Code is the longest surviving ancient Greek law code, inscribed in the Doric dialect of Crete on a curved stone wall at Gortyn around 450 BCE. Its twelve columns, written in alternating directions, set out rules on marriage, divorce, inheritance, adoption, property and the status of slaves and serfs, giving a rare view of family law in a Greek city.

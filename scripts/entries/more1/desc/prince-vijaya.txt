name: Prince Vijaya
type: person
dates: traditionally 543–505 BCE

Prince Vijaya is the legendary first king of Sri Lanka, said in the Pali chronicles to have been exiled from northern India and to have landed on the island with 700 followers on the day of the Buddha's death, traditionally 543 BCE. He married the Yakkha woman Kuveni, founded the settlement of Tambapanni and later took a bride from the Pandya country. The Sinhalese traditionally trace their origins to him.

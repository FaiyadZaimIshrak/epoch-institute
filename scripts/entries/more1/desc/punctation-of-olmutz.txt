name: Punctation of Olmütz
type: event (treaty)
dates: 1850

The Punctation of Olmütz was an agreement signed on 29 November 1850 at Olmütz in Moravia between Prussia and Austria, with Russian backing for Austria. Prussia abandoned its plan for an Erfurt Union of German states under its leadership and accepted the restoration of the German Confederation. Prussian nationalists called it the Humiliation of Olmütz, and it deepened the rivalry that led to war in 1866.

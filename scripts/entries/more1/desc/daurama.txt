name: Daurama
type: person
dates: none

Daurama is the queen of Daura in the Bayajidda legend, the origin story of the Hausa states. According to the legend she married the stranger Bayajidda after he killed the snake that guarded the Kusugu well, and their son Bawo fathered the founders of the seven Hausa kingdoms. Daura remembers her as one of a line of early queens.

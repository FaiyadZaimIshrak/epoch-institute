name: Mattavilasa Prahasana
type: work (text)
dates: 7th century

Mattavilasa Prahasana, 'The Farce of Drunken Sport', is a one-act Sanskrit comedy written in the early seventh century by the Pallava king Mahendravarman I. It mocks a drunken Kapalika ascetic, a greedy Buddhist monk and a Pashupata, who quarrel over a lost skull begging bowl in Kanchipuram. It is one of the earliest surviving Sanskrit farces and is still performed in the Kutiyattam tradition of Kerala.

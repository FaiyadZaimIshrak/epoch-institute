name: Banabhatta
type: person
dates: 7th century

Banabhatta was a 7th-century Sanskrit prose writer at the court of Harsha, king of Thanesar and Kannauj. His Harshacharita, a partly historical life of his patron, and his unfinished romance Kadambari, completed by his son, are classics of ornate Sanskrit prose. The Harshacharita is a main source for the rise of the Pushyabhuti dynasty and the wars that brought Harsha to power.

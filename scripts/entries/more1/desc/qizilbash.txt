name: Qizilbash
type: group
dates: none

The Qizilbash were a confederation of Turkmen tribes devoted to the Safavid Sufi order, named after their distinctive red headgear. They brought Ismail I to power in 1501 and formed the core military and political elite of the Safavid Empire. Their factionalism later led Shah Abbas I to counterbalance them with a corps of ghulam slave soldiers.

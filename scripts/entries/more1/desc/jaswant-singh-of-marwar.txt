name: Jaswant Singh of Marwar
type: person
dates: 1626–1678

Jaswant Singh was the Rathore ruler of Marwar, with its capital at Jodhpur, from 1638 to 1678 and a senior commander of the Mughal Empire. He served Shah Jahan and later Aurangzeb, campaigning in the Deccan and on the north-west frontier, where he died. When Aurangzeb tried to take over Marwar after his death, Durgadas Rathore led a long resistance on behalf of his posthumous son Ajit Singh.

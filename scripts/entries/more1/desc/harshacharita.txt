name: Harshacharita
type: work (text)
dates: 7th century

The Harshacharita ('Deeds of Harsha') is a Sanskrit prose biography of Harsha, king of Thanesar and Kannauj, written by his court poet Banabhatta in the 7th century. In eight chapters it tells of the poet's own life, the Pushyabhuti family, the deaths of Prabhakaravardhana and Rajyavardhana and Harsha's early campaigns, breaking off before his later reign. It is among the first historical biographies in Sanskrit literature.

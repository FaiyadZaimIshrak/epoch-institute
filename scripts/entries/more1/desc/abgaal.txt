name: Abgaal

dates: none

The Abgaal are a major Somali clan of the Hawiye clan family, belonging to its Hiraab branch and living mainly in and around Mogadishu and the middle Shabelle region. In the late 17th century they took part in the revolt that ended the Ajuran Sultanate's control of the coast and formed the Hiraab Imamate.

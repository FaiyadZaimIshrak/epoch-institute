name: Rashid Ali al-Gaylani
type: person
dates: 1892–1965

Rashid Ali al-Gaylani was an Iraqi nationalist politician who served several times as prime minister. In April 1941 he took power in a coup backed by four army officers known as the Golden Square and sought Axis support against Britain. Defeated in the Anglo-Iraqi War of May 1941, he fled and spent the rest of the Second World War in Germany.

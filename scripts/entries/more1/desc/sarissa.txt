name: Sarissa
type: term (weapon)
dates: none

The sarissa was a very long pike, several metres in length, carried by the infantry of the Macedonian phalanx. Introduced under Philip II, it allowed several ranks of soldiers to present their points to the enemy at once. It was central to the conquests of the Macedonian Empire and remained the core weapon of Hellenistic armies such as that of the Seleucid Empire.

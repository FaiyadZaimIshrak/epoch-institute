type: place (city)
dates: none

Akim Oda is a town in the Eastern Region of Ghana, in the valley of the Birim river. It became the capital of the Akan state of Akyem Kotoku in the 19th century and remains the seat of its paramount chief, the Kotokuhene. The surrounding district is known for cocoa farming and for the diamond fields of the Birim valley.

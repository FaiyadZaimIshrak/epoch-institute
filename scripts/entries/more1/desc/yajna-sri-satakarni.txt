name: Yajna Sri Satakarni
type: person
dates: late 2nd century CE

Yajna Sri Satakarni was one of the last powerful Satavahana kings, ruling in the late second century CE. He regained territory in the west from the Western Satraps, and his inscriptions and coins, including issues showing ships, come from both coasts of the Deccan. After his death the kingdom broke up among his successors and former feudatories.

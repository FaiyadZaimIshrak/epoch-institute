name: Ulises Heureaux
type: person
dates: 1845–1899

Ulises Heureaux was a Dominican general and dictator who dominated the Dominican Republic from 1882 until his assassination in 1899. A hero of the War of Restoration against Spain and a leader of Gregorio Luperón's Blue Party, he became president in 1882 and ruled almost continuously from 1887. He encouraged sugar plantations and railways, but heavy foreign borrowing left the country deeply in debt. He was shot at Moca in 1899.

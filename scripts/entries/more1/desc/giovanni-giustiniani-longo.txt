name: Giovanni Giustiniani Longo
type: person
dates: d. 1453

Giovanni Giustiniani Longo (died 1453) was a Genoese soldier from a leading family of Chios who brought some 700 men to Constantinople in January 1453. Emperor Constantine XI placed him in command of the defence of the land walls during the Ottoman siege. Gravely wounded on 29 May, the day the city fell, he was carried from the walls, which hastened the collapse of the defence, and he died of his wounds soon after.

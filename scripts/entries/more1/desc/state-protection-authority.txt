name: State Protection Authority
type: institution
dates: 1945–1956

The State Protection Authority, ÁVH, was the secret police of communist Hungary, known as the ÁVO until 1950. Under Mátyás Rákosi it carried out mass arrests, torture and the show trials of real and imagined opponents. Hated by the population, its members were targeted during the revolution of 1956, after which it was dissolved.

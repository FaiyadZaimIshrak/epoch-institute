name: Isthmian Games
type: event (other)
dates: none

The Isthmian Games were one of the four Panhellenic festivals of ancient Greece, held every two years near Corinth in honour of Poseidon, with victors crowned with pine or wild celery. In 196 BCE the Roman general Titus Quinctius Flamininus proclaimed the freedom of the Greek cities at the games, and after the Roman destruction of Corinth in 146 BCE they were run by Sicyon until the city was refounded.

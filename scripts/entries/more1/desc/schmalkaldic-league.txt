name: Schmalkaldic League
type: group
dates: 1531–1547

The Schmalkaldic League was a defensive alliance of Lutheran princes and imperial cities of the Holy Roman Empire, founded at Schmalkalden in 1531 and led by Philip of Hesse and John Frederick of Saxony. It resisted Emperor Charles V's efforts to restore Catholic unity until its defeat in the Schmalkaldic War, at Mühlberg in 1547. Members such as Constance and Lingen were then punished with loss of status or land.

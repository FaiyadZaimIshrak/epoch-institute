name: Kakusthavarma
type: person
dates: r. c. 435–455

Kakusthavarma was a king of the Kadamba dynasty of Banavasi in Karnataka in the 5th century, regarded as one of its most powerful rulers. The Talagunda inscription records that he married his daughters into royal houses, including, according to later readings, the Gupta and Vakataka dynasties. Under him the Kadambas reached their greatest extent.

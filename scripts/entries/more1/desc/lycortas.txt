name: Lycortas
type: person
dates: 2nd century BCE

Lycortas of Megalopolis was a statesman and general of the Achaean League in the early 2nd century BCE and the father of the historian Polybius. A close ally of Philopoemen, he led the league after Philopoemen was killed by the Messenians, recovering Messene in 182 BCE. He defended the league's independence against growing Roman interference in its affairs.

name: Ziusudra
type: person
dates: none

Ziusudra is the hero of the Sumerian flood story, a king of Shuruppak warned by the god Enki to build a great boat and save life from the deluge. Afterwards the gods granted him eternal life and settled him in Dilmun, where the sun rises. In the Akkadian Epic of Gilgamesh the same figure appears as Utnapishtim, whom Gilgamesh visits in search of immortality.

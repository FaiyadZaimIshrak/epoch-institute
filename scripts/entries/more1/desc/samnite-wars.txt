name: Samnite Wars
type: event (war)
dates: 343–290 BCE

The Samnite Wars were three wars fought between the Roman Republic and the Samnites of the southern Apennines between 343 and 290 BCE. Despite defeats such as the Caudine Forks in 321 BCE, Rome prevailed and, after the Battle of Sentinum in 295 BCE, became the dominant power in central and southern Italy. Neighbouring peoples who sided with the Samnites, such as the Aurunci, were crushed.

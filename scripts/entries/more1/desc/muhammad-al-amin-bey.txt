name: Muhammad al-Amin Bey
type: person
dates: 1881–1962

Muhammad al-Amin Bey, known as Lamine Bey, was the last ruler of the Husainid dynasty of Tunisia. Installed by the French in 1943 after Moncef Bey was deposed, he reigned as bey under the protectorate and became king when Tunisia gained independence in 1956. In 1957 the assembly led by Habib Bourguiba abolished the monarchy and placed him under house arrest.

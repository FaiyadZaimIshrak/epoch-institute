name: Ulugh Khan
type: person
dates: d. 1302

Ulugh Khan was a general of the Delhi Sultanate and a brother of Sultan Alauddin Khalji. With Nusrat Khan he invaded Gujarat in 1299, sacking Anahilavada and the temple of Somnath and forcing the Vaghela king Karna to flee. He also defeated a Mongol invasion in 1298 and took part in the siege of Ranthambore in 1301, dying soon afterwards in 1302.

name: Onake Obavva
type: person
dates: 18th century

Onake Obavva was a woman of Chitradurga in Karnataka, remembered for defending the fort against the army of Hyder Ali of Mysore in the 18th century. The wife of a guard, she is said to have found enemy soldiers creeping through a narrow opening in the walls and killed them one by one with an onake, a long wooden pestle. The opening is still shown as Obavvana Kindi, and she is honoured as a Kannada folk heroine.

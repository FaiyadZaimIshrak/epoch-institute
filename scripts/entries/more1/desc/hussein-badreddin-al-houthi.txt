name: Hussein Badreddin al-Houthi
type: person
dates: d. 2004

Hussein Badreddin al-Houthi was a Zaydi religious leader and former member of the Yemeni parliament who founded the movement that bears his family's name. From the Saada region he preached against Western and Saudi influence and the government of Ali Abdullah Saleh. He was killed by government forces in September 2004 in the first of six wars, and his brothers led the movement after him.

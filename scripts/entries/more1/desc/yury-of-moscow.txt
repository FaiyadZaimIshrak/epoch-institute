name: Yury of Moscow
type: person
dates: 1281–1325

Yury of Moscow was prince of Moscow from 1303 and the first Moscow prince to hold the grand princely throne of Vladimir. Married to Konchaka, a sister of Uzbek Khan of the Golden Horde, he obtained the khan's patent in 1317 and fought the princes of Tver, whose ruler Mikhail was executed at the Horde in 1318. He was himself killed at the Horde in 1325 by Mikhail's son Dmitry.

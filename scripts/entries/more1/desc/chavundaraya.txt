name: Chavundaraya
type: person
dates: 10th century

Chavundaraya was a minister and military commander of the Western Ganga kings Marasimha II and Rachamalla IV in the late 10th century. A devout Jain, he commissioned around 983 the giant statue of Bahubali, known as Gommateshwara, at Shravanabelagola. He also wrote the Chavundaraya Purana, one of the earliest surviving prose works in Kannada.

name: Juan Ginés de Sepúlveda
type: person
dates: 1494–1573

Juan Ginés de Sepúlveda was a Spanish humanist, philosopher and royal chronicler. Drawing on Aristotle's idea of natural slavery, he argued that the Spanish conquest of the Americas was just, because the indigenous peoples were barbarous and could be subjected for their own good. At the Valladolid debate of 1550–1551 he defended this view against Bartolomé de las Casas, but the crown did not allow his treatise Democrates alter to be printed.

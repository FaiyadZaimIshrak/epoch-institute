name: Paul Doumer
type: person
dates: 1857–1932

Paul Doumer was a French politician who served as governor-general of French Indochina from 1897 to 1902 and as president of France from 1931 until his assassination in 1932. In Indochina he centralised administration under the governor-general, set up a general budget financed by state monopolies on opium, salt and alcohol, and began major public works, including the Yunnan railway and the Hanoi bridge later named after him.

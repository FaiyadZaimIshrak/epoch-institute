name: Battle of Feyiase
type: event (battle)
dates: 1701

The Battle of Feyiase was fought in 1701 between the Asante and the Denkyira, the dominant Akan power of the region to which the Asante had paid tribute. The Asante, under Osei Tutu, won a decisive victory. The battle freed the Asante from Denkyira overlordship and established the Ashanti Empire as the leading power.

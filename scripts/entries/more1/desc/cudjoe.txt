name: Cudjoe
type: person
dates: fl. 1720s–1730s

Cudjoe was the leader of the Leeward Maroons of western Jamaica, communities of escaped enslaved Africans in the Cockpit Country. He led their resistance in the First Maroon War until, in 1739, he signed a treaty with the British governor Edward Trelawny that granted the Maroons land and self-government in return for returning runaways and aiding the colony against invasion.

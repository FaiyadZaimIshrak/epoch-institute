name: Altan Khan
type: person
dates: 1507–1582

Altan Khan was leader of the Tümed Mongols, a grandson of Dayan Khan who raided Ming China for decades. In 1571 he made peace with the Ming, gaining trade and a royal title, and he founded the city of Hohhot. In 1578 he met the Tibetan lama Sonam Gyatso and gave him the title Dalai Lama, beginning the conversion of the Mongols to Gelug Buddhism.

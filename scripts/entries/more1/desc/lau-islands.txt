name: Lau Islands

dates: none

The Lau Islands are a scattered group of about sixty islands in eastern Fiji, lying towards Tonga, with Lakeba and Vanua Balavu among the largest. Close contact with Tonga brought Tongan settlers and Wesleyan Christianity, and in the mid-19th century the Tongan noble Enele Maʻafu became the dominant power there. They form Fiji's Lau Province, whose chiefs, notably Ratu Sir Kamisese Mara, have been prominent in modern Fijian politics.

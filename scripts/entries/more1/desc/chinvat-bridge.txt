name: Chinvat Bridge
type: idea (concept)
dates: none

The Chinvat Bridge, or Bridge of the Requiter, is in Zoroastrianism the sifting bridge that separates the world of the living from the world of the dead, which all souls must cross upon death. According to the Bundahishn, it appears narrow to the wicked, whom the demon Chinnaphapast drags into the House of Lies, a place of eternal punishment, while for those whose good thoughts, words and deeds were many it is wide enough to cross, and the Daena leads the soul into the House of Song. Two four-eyed dogs guard it, as the Videvdat describes. It appears in the Yasna and the Vendidad, and bridges on early medieval Sogdian funerary couches have been identified with it.

name: Moamoria rebellion
type: event (revolt)
dates: 1769–1805

The Moamoria rebellion was a series of uprisings against the Ahom kings of Assam from 1769 to about 1805. It was led by disciples of the Moamara satra, a Vaishnavite monastery whose followers, many of them of low caste or tribal origin, had been persecuted by the court. The rebels twice seized the capital, Rangpur, and the conflict devastated the population and fatally weakened the kingdom.

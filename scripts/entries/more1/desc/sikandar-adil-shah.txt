name: Sikandar Adil Shah
type: person
dates: 1668–1700

Sikandar Adil Shah was the last sultan of Bijapur, placed on the throne as a small child in 1672. His reign was marked by feuding among the nobility, the loss of southern conquests to the Marathas and local commanders, and growing Mughal pressure. In 1686 Aurangzeb took Bijapur after a long siege, ending the Adil Shahi dynasty, and Sikandar died a prisoner in 1700.

name: Rurik dynasty
type: group
dates: 862–1598

The Rurikids were the ruling dynasty of Kievan Rus' and its successor principalities, traditionally descended from Rurik, a Varangian said to have settled at Novgorod around 862. Princes of the family shared out the towns of Rus' among themselves, and its branches ruled Kyiv, Galicia-Volhynia, Vladimir and Moscow. The Muscovite line ended with the death of Tsar Feodor I in 1598.

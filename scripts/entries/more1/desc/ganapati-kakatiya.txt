name: Ganapati (Kakatiya dynasty)
type: person
dates: r. 1199–1262

Ganapatideva was the longest-reigning ruler of the Kakatiya dynasty, governing from Warangal from 1199 to 1262. He united most Telugu-speaking lands, extending Kakatiya rule to the coastal Andhra country, and encouraged foreign trade through the port of Motupalli. His reign saw large irrigation tanks and temples built, and he was succeeded by his daughter Rudramadevi.

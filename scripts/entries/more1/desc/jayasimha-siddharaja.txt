name: Jayasimha Siddharaja
type: person
dates: r. 1092–1142

Jayasimha Siddharaja was a king of the Chaulukya dynasty of Gujarat who ruled from Anahilavada (Patan) from 1092 to 1142. He defeated the Paramaras of Malwa and extended Chaulukya power to its height. A patron of learning, he supported the Jain scholar Hemachandra, and built the Sahastralinga tank at Patan. He was succeeded by Kumarapala.

name: Hutu Manifesto
type: work (text)
dates: 1957

The Hutu Manifesto was a document written in March 1957 by nine Hutu intellectuals, among them Grégoire Kayibanda, and addressed to the Belgian colonial authorities in Rwanda. It denounced the Tutsi monopoly of political, economic and educational power and demanded advancement for the Hutu majority. It framed the conflict in ethnic terms and became a founding text of the Hutu movement that overthrew the monarchy.
